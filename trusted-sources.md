# Trusted sources

The assistant uses only the sources on this list. If a fact isn't available from one of them, the assistant says so and links to the official office that would know. Review this list yourself before testers see it, and add or remove outlets based on what you find.

## Rules for adding a source

A source belongs on the list if it meets one of these tests:

1. It is the government office that runs the election or makes the decision (U.S. Election Assistance Commission, Secretary of State, Arizona Legislature, county recorder or elections department, city clerk, city council).
2. It is a nonpartisan voter information group that does not endorse candidates.
3. It is a news outlet with a newsroom that covers Arizona government, publishes corrections, and separates news from opinion.

Opinion pieces, endorsements, campaign sites, party sites, and social media posts are never sources, even from outlets on this list.

## Known gaps

- Districts depend on the full address. A Tempe ZIP code can fall in more than one congressional or legislative district, so the assistant never guesses a district from a ZIP code. It sends users to https://my.arizona.vote to see their own districts and sample ballot.
- Small races (school boards, special districts, justices of the peace) often get little or no news coverage. For these, the assistant links to the Clean Elections guide and the county sample ballot and says that coverage is limited.
- Several Tier 3 outlets have paywalls. The assistant notes a paywall and offers a free source on the same story if one exists.
- This list covers Maricopa County only. Pima County and other counties need their own Tier 1 entries before the assistant supports them.
