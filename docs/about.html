About Precinctly

Young adults care. We help them follow through.

Precinctly exists so young adults in Arizona feel they have a say in the world around them, starting with the city they live in.

Our mission

Young adults care about their communities, but many don't feel they have the power to change them. So they miss the local elections that decide their rent, their commute, their schools, their tuition, and their health care.

Our goal is to give that power back. When you know what's on your ballot, when the deadlines are, and how a city council or school board race touches your daily life, your vote stops feeling small. Precinctly helps young adults see that they already have a say, and makes it easy to use it.

Sol, our assistant, works inside the AI tool you already use. It reaches out first instead of waiting for you to ask. All year, it sends a short news roundup from trusted sources. Before each election, it helps you check your address, learn what's on your ballot, and make a plan to vote.

What the research says

The problem isn't apathy. It's deadlines, moves, busy weeks, and too much noise. Each part of Sol answers one of these.

Why we built it this way

  • It reaches out first. Busy people won't think to ask a chatbot about a school board race, so Sol sends the reminder before they need it.
  • It helps you act. Studies show civics classes barely change who votes, but making a plan does. Sol focuses on the next step: register, request a ballot, or pick a day to vote.
  • It uses trusted sources only. AI chatbots still get voting questions wrong, so Sol answers from official election offices and a short list of trusted newsrooms, and links to them.

What Sol will never do

Sol will never recommend a candidate or ballot measure, guess at a rule, use sources outside its trusted list, track how you voted, or send updates you didn't sign up for.

Who we are

Precinctly is a student initiative from a course at Arizona State University. The student who built it has chosen to stay anonymous.

No one makes money from this project. There are no ads, no paid plans, and no data to sell. Precinctly doesn't collect your answers. They stay in your browser.

Precinctly isn't an official ASU or government service. For official election information, go to Arizona.Vote or your county elections office.
