About Precinctly

You have more power than you think.

The people you elect set your rent, your tuition, and the laws you live under. Precinctly makes sure you never miss the chance to choose them, starting in the city you call home.

Our mission

Young adults care about their communities, but many don't feel they have the power to change them. So they miss the local and national elections that choose the leaders who shape their communities and the world they'll inherit. Those leaders decide their rent, their commute, their schools, their tuition, and their health care.

Our goal is to give that power back. When you know what's on your ballot, when the deadlines are, and how the people you elect shape your rent, your paycheck, and the world you live in, your vote stops feeling small. Precinctly helps young adults see that they already have a say, and makes it easy to use it.

Sol, our assistant, works inside the AI tool you already use. It reaches out first instead of waiting for you to ask. All year, it sends a short news roundup from trusted sources. Before each election, it helps you check your address, learn what's on your ballot, and make a plan to vote.

What the research says

The problem isn't apathy. It's deadlines, moves, busy weeks, and too much noise. Each part of Sol answers one of these.

Why we built it this way

  • It reaches out first. Busy people don't stop to look up election deadlines, so Sol sends the reminder before they need it.
  • It helps you act. Studies show civics classes barely change who votes, but making a plan does. Sol focuses on the next step: register, request a ballot, or pick a day to vote.
  • It uses trusted sources only. AI chatbots still get voting questions wrong, so Sol answers from official election offices and a short list of trusted newsrooms, and links to them.

What Sol will never do

Sol will never recommend a candidate or ballot measure, guess at a rule, use sources outside its trusted list, track how you voted, or send updates you didn't sign up for.

Who we are

Built by a student so young Arizonans can shape the world they live in.

Precinctly is a student initiative from a course at Arizona State University. The student who built it has chosen to stay anonymous. No one makes money from it. There are no ads, no paid plans, and no data to sell. Your answers stay in your browser.

Precinctly isn't an official ASU or government service. For official election information, go to Arizona.Vote or your county elections office.
